Layman Process Guide

🥤 How the KPI Analyzer Works

A super simple, 6-step walkthrough showing what happens to your data from Excel upload to 1-page PDF reports.

📂
Step 1 of 6
Upload Your Excel Sales Report
Drag and drop your monthly sales file (.xlsx) onto the home screen.
💡 What happens here: You provide your sales spreadsheet (such as SMO July Report.xlsx). If you don't have a file ready, you can click the quick demo button to test with sample data immediately.
⚙️
Step 2 of 6
Choose Your Target Standards
Set what counts as Green (Good), Yellow (Average), and Red (Low).
💡 What happens here: In the sidebar, you set target levels (e.g., 100% for Green sales goal, 85% for Yellow). The app validates your inputs so Yellow is never set higher than Green.
🧹
Step 3 of 6
Automatic Calculations & Data Cleaning
The app reads all 43 sales officers, calculates percentages, and separates totals.
💡 What happens here: The program scans rows to find headings automatically, handles missing values, normalizes decimals (e.g. 0.20 ➔ 20%), separates summary rows, and assigns status colors (Green, Yellow, Red) to all sales officers.
📊
Step 4 of 6
Explore Simple Team Bar Charts
See Target vs Actual Sales and overall health in clear, layman-friendly charts.
💡 What happens here: No complex scatter plots! You get simple horizontal bar charts showing target quota (Gray) vs sales made (Blue), plus counts of sales officers in Green, Yellow, and Red performance zones.
👤
Step 5 of 6
Inspect Individual Sales Officer (SMO) Profile
Read their 6-paragraph summary, exact deficits, and action plan.
💡 What happens here: Pick any salesperson (like SAAD SADDIQUE) to see their exact 6-paragraph diagnostic report, a list of Where We Are Low (specific percentage gaps), and What To Enhance (coaching action steps).
📄
Step 6 of 6
Download 1-Page PDF & Excel Reports
Download printable PDF report cards or the entire team in a ZIP file.
💡 What happens here: Click one button to download a high-resolution, print-ready 1-page A4 PDF report card, download a ZIP archive with individual PDFs for all sales officers, or download a complete Excel summary workbook.
