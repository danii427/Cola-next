📊 Layman Visual Analytics Suite
Simple, easy-to-understand bar charts designed for quick executive decision-making. No complex scatter plots.
🎯 1. Target vs Actual Sales (By Distributor)
💡 What this represents:
• Gray Bar: Monthly Target Quota (Cases).
• Blue Bar: Actual Delivered Sales (Cases).
• If Blue exceeds Gray, that distribution territory exceeded its quota!
🚦 2. Team Performance Health Zones
💡 What this represents:
• Green (≥100%): Star performers meeting or exceeding quota.
• Yellow (85-99%): Stable performers within reach of goal.
• Red (<85%): Sales officers requiring urgent field coaching.
🏆 3. Top Champions vs Critical Watchlist (Achievement %)
💡 What this represents:
• Compares the highest-achieving sales officers against those with highest deficits.
• Green bars represent champions (>100%), Red bars represent critical recovery priorities.
📞 4. Route Visit & Booking Funnel (Monthly Total)
💡 What this represents:
• Step 1 (Gray): Planned Route Calls scheduled in advance.
• Step 2 (Blue): Actual Storefront Visits completed.
• Step 3 (Green): Productive Visits where a retailer placed an order.
🚚 5. Order Delivery Fulfillment (Cases)
💡 What this represents:
• Blue Bar: Total Ordered Cases booked by sales team.
• Green Bar: Successfully Delivered Cases.
• Red Bar: Undelivered / Cancelled Cases (distribution drop-offs).
📍 6. Geolocation & GPS Radius Accuracy (%)
💡 What this represents:
• Measures whether check-ins occurred inside the retailer store (PJP Radius).
• Green (≥90%): High route compliance.
• Red (<75%): Off-radius check-ins needing audit.
